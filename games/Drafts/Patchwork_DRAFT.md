# Patchwork
## How to Win
- Have the *Most Buttons* once both players are "out of time" on the track.

## Setup
- Each player:
	- Take a blank quilt board
	- Put player pawn at start of *time track board*
	- Take 5 buttons from bank
- Put the five *1x1 tiles* on their spots on the *time track board*
- Make a circle of all patches around *time track board*
- Place *wood marker* immediately clockwise of *1x2 patch*

## Rounds
### Player Turn
- Whichever Player piece is behind or on top takes a turn.
### EITHER:
#### Buy a Patch
1. Buy one of the next 3 patches for sale (clockwise from *wood marker*)
2. Move the *wood marker* to where that patch was
3. Place patch on your quilt
4. Pay the buttons to bank, and move the time on time tracking board
	- Get any *button payout* and *1x1 patch* you passed from the *time track board*
#### OR Get Paid Buttons
1. Move your piece down *time track* until you are one space directly in front of opponent (if opponent is in final space, move to the final space)
2. For every space you moved, take 1 button (ex: 3 spaces pays 3 buttons)
3. If you passed a button, do *button payout*

### Moving on Time Track
- If you pass a button, do *button payout*
	- Count buttons on tiles on your quilt, including any patch you may have just purchased. For each button on the tiles, take a button from the bank
- If you pass a *1x1 tile*, pick it up and immediately place it on your quilt
- If you land on the same space as opponent, your piece goes on top and will go again.
- If you have more time and would overshoot the final spot, just stop on the final spot.
- Buttons and *1x1 patches* on *time track* are not spaces, move over them to get their bonus.

## End of Game & Scoring
- Once both players are "out of time" and in final space on *time tracking board*, count score
- Highest score wins
	- Every button you have (including 7x7 bonus) is +1 point
	- Every uncovered space on your quilt is -2 points
