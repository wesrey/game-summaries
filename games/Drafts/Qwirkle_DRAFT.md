# Qwirkle
## Useful to Remember:
* SIX is a key number:
	* Hand size
	* Six colors and Six shapes
	* Max Line Length (a Qwirkle)
	* Qwirkle bonus score (6 length + 6 bonus)
	* Final turn Bonus for player who plays all final tiles
- Total Tile Count: 6 colors; 6 shapes; 3 copies of each tile; 6*6*3=108 tiles

## How to Win
- Have the *Most Points* when someone has run completely out of tiles, and the bag is empty

## Setup
- Each player draws SIX tiles
- Starting Player:
	- Whoever can play the longest line (all same Color or Shape) plays it first
	- 	Tie? Oldest player breaks a tie, for some reason

## Rounds
### Player Turn
1. Place one or more tiles anywhere in a line as long as all tiles in the line are touching
	1. A line is same-COLOR or same-SHAPE, with no tiles repeating (So: max length SIX)
2. Add up your score for those tiles
3. Draw back up to SIX tiles, or the most possible (if bag doesn't have enough left)

## Scoring
- Count the length of each line you added to, re-counting a tile if it's used in both horizontal and vertical lines
- If a line is SIX, it's a Qwirkle. Score the six with another SIX bonus points (and yell "Qwirkle!")
	- Qwirkle lines are 12 points

### End of Game
- When a player has played all of their tiles, and the bag is empty, that player scores SIX bonus points for triggering end of game
- Highest score wins
