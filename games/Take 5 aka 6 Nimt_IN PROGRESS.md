# Take 5 / 6 Nimmt
104 Cards

## Setup
Deal everyone 10 cards
Place 4 cards faceup on the table, aligned vertically

## How to Win
When someone gets 66 "bulls" at the end of a round, the game is over. Least "bulls" wins.
