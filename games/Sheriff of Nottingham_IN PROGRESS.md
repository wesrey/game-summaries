# Sheriff of Nottingham

## Setup

### Prepare the Decks
- Remove 12 Royal Goods (Contraband that isn't Mead, Crossbow, Silk, Pepper) for a "Basic" game
- Remove "4+" (seen in bottom right) cards for 3 player game
- Put deck in middle of 3-segment card tray, facedown
- Put top 5 cards face-up in right segment of card tray to form Discard Pile A
- Put next top 5 cards face-up in left segment of card tray for Discard Pile B

### Player Setup
- Give each player their colored board and bag
- Give each player 50 coins (one 20, five 5s, and five 1s)
- Deal 6 cards to each player

- First player is person holding the most actual cash on them

## Overview
Legal Goods are Green (Apple, Bread, Cheese, Chicken)
Contraband is Red (Mead, Crossbow, Silk, Pepper)
Royal Goods are also Contraband (with golden banner at the bottom), and are not recommended for first game.
Card Distribution is on the back of the Rulebook.
Everyone will take turns being the Sheriff. The Sheriff doesn't participate in the Round except for Phase 4 where they decide to open bags or not.

## Players' Round
In turn order, starting with the player to the left of the Sheriff, everyone participates in the following Rounds. The Sheriff only participates in Round 4.

## Phase 1 - The Market Phase
Player picks up to 5 cards in their hand to set aside, face down, to replace.
Player then draws that same number of cards from discard piles and draw deck.
Player can draw from top of either discard pile, but if they ever begin to draw from Draw deck, they must continue to draw from Draw deck for the remainder.
Player then places their previously set-aside pile of cards into one of the two discard piles as a group (they can order them how they please).

## Game End
When everyone has been the Sheriff twice (thrice for a 3-player game), the game is over and scores are tallied.
