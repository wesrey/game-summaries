# Splendor

## How to Win
Everyone gets the same number of turns. When a player reaches `15` points in a round, finish the round, then the highest scoring player Wins!
### Tie-Breaker
Fewest purchased cards

## Setup
1. Deal 4 cards faceup from each of the 3 decks
2. Reveal as many `Noble Tiles` as there are **Players + 1**.
	- Ex: 4 player game: Reveal **5 `Noble Tiles`**
3. Place the poker chips in their respective stacks.
	- *2 Players*: Use 4 gems. **Remove 3 tokens of each gem** (not gold)
	- *3 Players*: Use 5 gems. **Remove 2 tokens of each gem** (not gold)
	- 4-5 players, use all 7 gems

## Start of Game
Youngest Player starts

## Player Turn
### Choose one of the following four options:
1. Take 3 different gem tokens
2. Take 2 of the same gem token (*there must be 4 tokens*)
3. Reserve 1 card AND take 1 gold token (*max 3 reserved cards*)
4. Purchase a face-up card, or a previously reserved card

### End of Turn
- Discard down to 10 tokens.
- Claim one `Noble Tile`, if possible.

## Miscellaneous Rules
### Reserving Cards
- Max 3 Reserved Cards.
- The only way to get rid of a reserved card is to purchase it.
- If there are no `Gold Token`s left, you can still reserve a card.
### Noble Tiles
- At the end of a player's turn, if they met the requirements of at least one `Noble Tile`, they MUST claim one (and only one) `Noble Tile`. 
- This is a "free action", and required.
### Gold Tokens
- `Gold Token`s act as a "Wild" token
- The only way to obtain a `Gold Token` is by reserving cards
